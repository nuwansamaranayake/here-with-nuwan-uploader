Here With Nuwan Uploader

Here With Nuwan Uploader is a private tool. It runs on the channel owner's own computer and uploads videos to the Here With Nuwan YouTube channel.

What it does with a Google account: it uploads the owner's videos, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and playlists, and reads the status of those videos. It works only on the channel of the person who signs in.

The tool is not offered to the public. It has one user, the channel owner.
